Hi! I'm Camilla Baumgarten. I am a curious creative with a love for digital design and visual storytelling. My work moves between illustration, motion and branding.

With a background in art, culture, branding and marketing, I've spent the past years exploring visual communication across different fields: From galleries and cultural institutions to design projects and the tech scene.

These experiences shaped my interest in the relationship between visual expression and communication, and led me to pursue a degree in Multimedia Design to further develop my creative and technical skills.

I'm drawn to projects that leave room for curiosity, experimentation and unexpected ideas. Whether working with illustration, building a visual identity or bringing a story to life through motion, I enjoy exploring new ways to communicate visually.
